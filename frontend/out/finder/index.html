StyleTrail Finder

A bookmark that works on your phone and your computer, no app or extension needed. On a Myntra page you opened from your results, tap it: it finds your piece on the page, highlights it, and adds its link to your results. On a Myntra product page, it saves the price to that product’s price history.

1. Copy the Finder

StyleTrail FinderOn a computer you can drag this button to your bookmarks bar instead.

2. Save it as a bookmark

Android, Chrome

  1. On this page, tap ⋮ then the ☆ star to bookmark it.
  2. Tap Edit. Name it Finder.
  3. Clear the URL, paste the Finder code, and save.

iPhone, Safari

  1. Tap Share, then Add Bookmark, and save.
  2. Open Bookmarks, tap Edit, then this bookmark.
  3. Name it Finder, replace the address with the Finder code, and tap Done.

3. Use it on Myntra

  1. Search with your photo on StyleTrail, then open a link from Spotted on. If Myntra tries to open its app, choose to stay in the browser.
  2. Android: tap the address bar, type Finder, and tap the bookmark that appears. iPhone / computer: open your bookmarks and tap Finder.
  3. Scroll the page. Your piece gets a pink Your piece label and is added to your results.

Found it in the Myntra app instead? Share the product, copy the link, and paste it into “Found it yourself?” on your results page.
